Our Story

From a Small Garden to Georgetown's Finest Organic Shop

GreenLeaf Organics was born in 2018 from a simple belief: that every family in Georgetown deserves access to genuinely fresh, chemical-free food. Our founder, Maya Persaud, grew up on her grandmother's farm in the Demerara region, where the soil was rich and the food tasted like it should.

When Maya moved to Georgetown, she noticed that organic, locally grown produce was nearly impossible to find. Supermarket shelves were stocked with imported, pesticide-treated food — often days old by the time it reached customers. She decided to change that.

Today, GreenLeaf Organics partners with eight certified organic farms across Demerara, Berbice and Essequibo. We bring their harvest directly to our Regent Street shop and deliver twice a week to homes across Georgetown — always fresh, always honest, always organic.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🌎

Environmental Responsibility

We use 100% compostable packaging and work only with farms that practise sustainable agriculture — protecting Guyana's soil, water and biodiversity for future generations.

🤝

Fair Farmer Partnerships

We pay our farm partners above market rate and guarantee advance purchase commitments so they can plan confidently — no middlemen, no exploitation.

🔬

Uncompromising Quality

Every batch undergoes a physical quality check before leaving the farm. We reject anything below standard — you'll never find a tired or chemically-treated product at GreenLeaf.

🏘️

Community First

We donate surplus produce to the Georgetown Community Kitchen each week and run quarterly organic gardening workshops open to all Georgetown residents — free of charge.
